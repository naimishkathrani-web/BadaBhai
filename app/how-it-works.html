The POC · hackathon build

A call that works today

What we built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon: a real phone call on an ordinary feature phone. No app. No smartphone required. Dial, ask in Hindi or English, and hear a short answer.

Open /dashboard during a call to see live latency breakdown. Backup demo ↗

Call path · feature phone / basic phone

1 · CallerFeature phone or ordinary phone dials the Exotel number. This is a normal phone call, not a browser call.
2 · TelephonyExotel ordinary phone call → Windows Server. One call at a time.
3 · HearMeta Voice Transcribe — Hindi or English on a noisy phone line
4 · AnswerMuse Wonder 1.0 — short spoken answer; Meta web search for a public office, address, or phone
5 · SpeakIndian voice, no wait tone · auto Hindi/English · speak over the answer to interrupt

Operator dashboard

Open /dashboard before you call. Words, language, and timings appear only while the call is open, then they are gone.

Stack today

  • Exotel trial line 07948504917, active till about 9 Nov. If the line is down: /backup-demo
  • FastAPI + Uvicorn on Windows Server 2022, Nginx TLS
  • One simultaneous call
  • Meta Voice Transcribe · Muse Wonder 1.0 · Indian voice (Meta MMS only as fallback)
  • Future ideas such as SAM are not on today’s call

Limits today

  • Hindi / English demo only
  • Generic Q&A — no personal account access
  • No eligibility determination
  • No Aadhaar / OTP / PIN collection
  • Not a government helpline
  • One call at a time; free trial line may end around 9 Nov

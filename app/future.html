What we might pick up next

The live demo is a useful phone conversation. The first thing we would build on top of it is practical help for people who already have a basic phone and little else.

Instant impact: nearby work, by voice

A worker calls, says the kind of work and the area, and hears a few nearby leads from consented or official sources: place, date, and who posted. Posts stay unverified. The caller is told not to pay anyone for a job, and to check a labour office or e-Shram for real benefits. Area would be spoken by the caller only if they choose to say it. This is the idea we would pick up next. It is not running on the demo line today.

After that

Where the same phone call could go

Hundreds of millions of basic phones never reach today's AI apps. The same ordinary call could later explain school topics, public schemes in regional languages, replace long phone menus, and take a village order.

01

Everyday topics

Maths, science, how-to on a feature phone — Hindi, English, Swahili, Bahasa Indonesia, and more as validated.

02

Govt reach

Verified sources for programs like Ayushman Bharat ₹5 lakh medical insurance — many needy families in Gujarat, UP, Bengal have not heard (Gujarati, Bhojpuri, Bengali). Same for gig workers on feature phones, not LinkedIn.

03

More languages

Later, the same call could speak Gujarati, Bhojpuri, Bengali, Tamil, Swahili, and Bahasa. Today's demo is Hindi and English only.

04

Replace IVR

Caller says “My bill seems too high” and reaches the right self-service or staff path. Account changes still need auth.

05

Village orders

Voice orders for groceries or farm supplies connected to logistics beyond cities. Location asked today; tower triangulation later with operator tie-ups.

06

Live translation

Help a Hindi-speaking farmer talk with an English-speaking buyer — if accuracy, latency, and consent are handled.

Everything on this page is a next step, not a feature of the live call. BadaBhai today is an independent hackathon prototype, not a Meta or government service.

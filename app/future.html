Platform Play: Where this could go next

About 2.1B active feature phones / basic phones; roughly 210M sold in 2024, predominantly Asia Pacific and Sub-Saharan Africa. Most AI never reaches them. BadaBhai shows how voice on an ordinary call could. Works on ordinary phone (no app required). WhatsApp option for those who have it, but not required.

01

Everyday topics

Maths, science, how-to on a feature phone — Hindi, English, Swahili, Bahasa Indonesia, and more as validated.

02

Govt reach

Verified sources for programs like Ayushman Bharat ₹5 lakh medical insurance — many needy families in Gujarat, UP, Bengal have not heard (Gujarati, Bhojpuri, Bengali). Same for gig workers on feature phones, not LinkedIn.

03

Voice-token model

Opt-in anonymized speech in Bhojpuri, Tamil, Marathi, Swahili, Hausa, Bahasa, Tagalog could inform Meta research on language control & prosody. Demo does not use calls for training. Future research only with separate opt-in, strong anonymization, PII removal, local governance. Could inform Meta research.

04

Replace IVR

Caller says “My bill seems too high” and reaches the right self-service or staff path. Account changes still need auth.

05

Village orders

Voice orders for groceries or farm supplies connected to logistics beyond cities. Location asked today; tower triangulation later with operator tie-ups.

06

Live translation

Help a Hindi-speaking farmer talk with an English-speaking buyer — if accuracy, latency, and consent are handled.

Voice-token / research disclaimer: Demo does not use calls for training. Future research only with separate opt-in, strong anonymization, PII removal, local governance. Could inform Meta research. These futures require approvals, infrastructure, consent, and local partnerships. BadaBhai today is an independent hackathon prototype, not a Meta or government service.

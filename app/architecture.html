How we built this BadaBhai POC for Meta Hackathon

Following Meta Hackathon guidelines: multi-model use · responsible AI · architecture. Feature-phone / basic phone / ordinary phone first. Real telephony — Exotel POTS, not WebRTC. 1 concurrent call. We will build with MCA/BTech interns on this same stack.

2. Architecture

flowchart LR A["Feature / basic / ordinary phone call\n(POTS)"] --> B["Exotel POTS\n1 concurrent call\nWindows Server 2022"] B --> C["Meta Voice Transcribe\nSTT ~800ms"] C --> D["Muse Spark 1.1\nreasoning 2-5s"] D --> E["Meta TTS\n+ waiting tone"] E --> F["Caller hears answer\nKeypad 1/2/3/9"] G["Operator Dashboard\nLive transcript\nSTT/Spark/TTS timing"] --- B H["Future data layer\nFB Groups + IG Reels + WhatsApp posts\nLocation · Date · Contact\n+ disclaimer"] --- D I["Later\nMore Indian languages\nOnly after a real partner\nNot part of this call"] --- D
Live Desk shows the words and timings only while the call is open. Phone numbers stay hidden. The call is not saved. No Aadhaar, OTP, or bank PIN. No personal account access.

3. Responsible AI & guidelines

  • No Aadhaar / OTP / PIN / bank collection — system prompt blocks these asks
  • No personal account access; no eligibility determination · General guidance only
  • Live Desk is only a window on the open call. Words are not saved after the call ends.
  • General guidance. For personal rules, it points to an official office.
  • A future job helper would say posts are public and unverified, and not to pay for a job.
  • Temporary Exotel number is not toll-free, not a government helpline; international charges depend on carrier · trial till ~9 Nov · backup /backup-demo
  • 1 concurrent call + 3–8 sec thinking disclosed with waiting tone — latency is not hidden
  • Works on ordinary phone (no app required). WhatsApp option for those who have it, but not required.

Phases require approvals, partnerships, consent, and infrastructure. Independent prototype — not a Meta or government service.

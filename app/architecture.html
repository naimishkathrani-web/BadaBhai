How we built this BadaBhai POC

An ordinary phone call into Meta models. No app. Feature phone first. One concurrent call. Built for judges to dial and hear.

1. How a call moves

  1. The caller dials the Exotel number on a feature phone or any ordinary phone.
  2. Exotel Voicebot opens a WebSocket to our app and streams 8 kHz telephone audio both ways.
  3. We resample that audio and send it to Meta Muse Voice Transcribe for Hindi or English speech-to-text.
  4. The final transcript goes to Muse Spark 1.3. Everyday questions get a short answer. A public office, address, or phone question uses Meta web search on the Responses API.
  5. An Indian neural voice speaks the answer back through Exotel. There is no wait tone. The caller can press 1, 2, 3, or 9, or speak over the answer.
  6. Live Desk at /dashboard shows the words and timings only while the call is open. Nothing is saved.

2. Meta models in this POC

Model / layerRole in BadaBhai
Meta Voice TranscribeHears the caller on a noisy phone line. Hindi and English. Estimate about 0.8s after the caller stops speaking.
Muse Spark 1.3Writes the spoken answer. Short replies for everyday questions. Meta web search for a public office, address, or phone number; speaks only what that search found.
Indian voiceSpeaks Hindi or Indian English on the phone. Meta MMS is only an offline fallback. No wait tone.
Exotel VoicebotOrdinary phone audio in and out. Not a browser call. One call at a time on this demo.
Not in today’s callSAM, Muse Image, and Muse Wonder are not used on the live line. Interns could later learn with Muse Code on this same phone path.

3. Architecture diagram

Live Desk shows words only while the call is open. Phone numbers stay hidden. The call is not saved. No Aadhaar, OTP, or bank PIN. The live call does not ask for location.

4. Host for this POC

This demo runs on one Windows Server 2022 machine with about 16 GB RAM, Nginx for TLS, and one Uvicorn process. There is no GPU on this host. Meta Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark run in Meta’s cloud. The Indian voice is synthesized on the server. Answers can take a few seconds, especially when Meta web search is used. That pause is expected for this POC. A faster private GPU box was not affordable for this hackathon.

5. Responsible AI

  • No Aadhaar, OTP, PIN, or bank details. The system prompt blocks those asks.
  • No personal account access and no eligibility decisions. General guidance only.
  • Live Desk is only a window on the open call. Words are not saved after the call ends.
  • If Meta web search does not confirm a public address or phone number, the voice says so. It does not invent one.
  • A future job helper would use only consented or official sources, say posts are unverified, and tell people not to pay for a job.
  • Temporary Exotel number is not toll-free and not a government helpline. Trial till about 9 Nov. Backup: /backup-demo.
  • One call at a time. Silence or noise gets a short “please ask again.”

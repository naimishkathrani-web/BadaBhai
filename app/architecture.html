How we built this BadaBhai POC for Meta Hackathon

Following Meta Hackathon guidelines: multi-model use · responsible AI · architecture. Feature-phone / basic phone / ordinary phone first. Real telephony — Exotel POTS, not WebRTC. 1 concurrent call. We will build with MCA/BTech interns on this same stack.

1. Meta multi-model use

Model / layerRole in BadaBhai
Meta Voice TranscribeTurns noisy Hindi or English phone audio into text. Estimate about 0.8s after the caller stops speaking.
Muse Spark 1.3Writes a short spoken answer. Everyday questions stay short. A public office, address, or phone number uses Meta web search, and the voice says only what that search found.
Indian voiceSpeaks the answer on the phone line in Indian English or Hindi. Meta MMS is only an offline fallback. No wait tone. The caller can speak over an answer or press 9 to interrupt.
Exotel telephonyOrdinary phone call, not a browser call. One call at a time. Press 1 for what it can do, 2 for English, 3 for Hindi, or 9 to stop.
Later, not liveSAM, Muse Image, and Muse Wonder are not on the call today. A future WhatsApp photo path could use SAM. Interns could learn with Muse Code.

2. Architecture

flowchart LR A["Feature / basic / ordinary phone call\n(POTS)"] --> B["Exotel POTS\n1 concurrent call\nWindows Server 2022"] B --> C["Meta Voice Transcribe"] C --> D["Muse Spark 1.3\nshort answer or web search"] D --> E["Indian voice"] E --> F["Caller hears answer\nKeypad 1/2/3/9"] G["Live Desk\nwords only while the call is open"] --- B H["Later, not in this call\nnearby work ideas"] --- D I["Later\nMore Indian languages"] --- D
Live Desk shows the words and timings only while the call is open. Phone numbers stay hidden. The call is not saved. No Aadhaar, OTP, or bank PIN. No personal account access. The live call does not ask for location.

3. Responsible AI & guidelines

  • No Aadhaar / OTP / PIN / bank collection — system prompt blocks these asks
  • No personal account access; no eligibility determination · General guidance only
  • Live Desk is only a window on the open call. Words are not saved after the call ends.
  • General guidance. For personal rules, it points to an official office.
  • If the search does not confirm a public address or phone number, the voice says so. It does not invent one.
  • A future job helper would use only consented or official sources, say posts are unverified, and tell people not to pay for a job.
  • Temporary Exotel number is not toll-free and not a government helpline. Trial till about 9 Nov. Backup: /backup-demo.
  • One call at a time. A short pause while the answer is prepared is normal. Silence or noise gets a short “please ask again.”

4. Phased roadmap

Phase 0 · POC today

Exotel trial 07948504917 (active till ~9 Nov) · FastAPI on Windows Server 2022 · Meta speech-to-text and Muse Spark · Indian voice · Hindi and English · ordinary phone · one call · Live Desk · backup walkthrough at /backup-demo

Phase 1 · Explore next

Nearby work by voice for a Rajkot and Ahmedabad pilot, only from consented or official sources, with a clear “do not pay for a job” line and e-Shram guidance. Location would be asked on the call only if the person chooses to say it. Nothing from the call is saved today.

Phase 2 · Later

Official-source scheme explainers in more Indian languages, more phone lines, and a move off a single server. Needs partners and approvals.

Phase 3 · Later still

Simpler phone menus, village voice orders, and a live translation bridge. None of this runs on the demo line today.

Phases require approvals, partnerships, consent, and infrastructure. Independent prototype — not a Meta or government service.

Commitment

Why I’m building this, and how

I grew up seeing that the best government efforts often don’t reach the last person because they are published in Hindi or English. Ayushman Bharat offers ₹5 lakh medical insurance, yet many needy families in Gujarat, UP, and Bengal have not even heard of it — their languages are Gujarati, Bhojpuri, Bengali. The same gap hits gig workers on feature phones / basic phones / ordinary phones, not LinkedIn. We will build with MCA/BTech interns learning Meta models on real phone infra.

1

Gig-work discovery pilot · Rajkot & Ahmedabad

Build a voice helper that finds nearby work from consented or official sources, plus an optional WhatsApp number for contractors who share requirements by voice note in Hindi or Gujarati. Seekers can call from any ordinary phone. Show a few options with posted date and contact, with a clear disclaimer that posts are unverified and that nobody should pay for a job. This is a next step, not part of today’s call.

2

Government schemes in every regional language

Build voice explainers for schemes like Ayushman Bharat ₹5 lakh medical insurance, PM-KISAN, and e-Shram in Gujarati, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Bhojpuri, and more — always citing the official source — so a person can call and hear, in their own language, what help exists and where to go. Many needy families in Gujarat, UP, and Bengal have not heard of Ayushman — language is the gap.

3

Build with MCA / BTech students as interns

Run this as a live internship for MCA and BTech students from Rajkot and Gujarat colleges. They learn to build with Meta models — Muse Spark, Voice Transcribe, TTS, and later SAM / Glimmer / Muse Code — on real phone infrastructure, not only classroom projects. They gain experience; I gain speed for regional-language coverage; the project stays rooted in the community it serves.

If BadaBhai wins any prize in the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, I will invest half of it into: (1) gig-work pilot Rajkot & Ahmedabad, (2) govt schemes in regional languages, and (3) build with MCA/BTech interns learning Meta models on real phone infra. BadaBhai today is an independent hackathon prototype — not a Meta or government service.

Instant Impact: Jobs for Gig Workers

Start: Feature phone call (no app) → BadaBhai asks location (asked today, tower triangulation later with operator tie-ups), preference, qualification → searches public job requirements from Facebook Groups + Instagram Reels (location, date, contact Meta already has). Suggested addition for greater impact (if Meta partners): Add 2 separate WhatsApp numbers — one for gig workers to search jobs by preference, one for employers/contractors to post requirements via voice note in Hindi/Gujarati. This would create a 3rd Meta gig-work finder alongside FB and IG, for people who can’t read or have no data, with both seeker and poster sides. Disclaimer: public posts by individuals, not verified by Meta/BadaBhai, info only, don’t pay for a job, plus govt labour benefits e-Shram.

Instant Impact: Jobs for gig workers

Start with the feature phone / basic phone / ordinary phone. A worker calls AI on an ordinary phone (no app required). WhatsApp option for those who have it, but not required. AI asks location (asked today, tower triangulation later with operator tie-ups), preference, and qualification, then searches public Facebook Groups and Instagram Reels — so people who can’t read app UIs or don’t have data still hear nearby work options.

The problem

Hundreds of millions of gig workers use feature phones / basic phones. Job posts already appear daily in Facebook Groups (“Delhi Labour Chowk Jobs”, “Mumbai Driver Requirement”) and Instagram Reels (“Need 2 boys for packing Rajkot 500/day”). Location, date, and contact often exist — but there is no voice interface for people who can’t read or have no smartphone data plan. Same gap: gig workers on feature phones, not LinkedIn — and many needy families in Gujarat, UP, Bengal have not heard of Ayushman Bharat ₹5 lakh medical insurance (languages Gujarati, Bhojpuri, Bengali).

Phase A · Feature-phone call (primary)

1 · Call from feature / basic phone

Worker dials an ordinary number (same class of line as today’s Exotel POC). No app. WhatsApp not required for this path.

2 · AI asks three things

Location (asked today, tower triangulation later with operator tie-ups), work preference (packing, driving, housekeeping…), and qualification / willingness — in Hindi or local language.

3 · Search & speak results

AI searches public job requirements from Facebook Groups + Instagram Reels (location, date, contact Meta already has) and reads 3–4 options with posted date and group/source.

Safety disclaimer. These are public posts by individuals. Posted dates are shown. They are not verified or guaranteed by Meta or BadaBhai. For information only. Do not pay anyone for a job. Verify details before you go. Also surface relevant labour benefits such as e-Shram.
Suggested addition for greater impact (if Meta partners): Add 2 separate WhatsApp numbers — one for gig workers to search jobs by preference, one for employers/contractors to post requirements via voice note in Hindi/Gujarati. This would create a 3rd Meta gig-work finder alongside FB and IG, for people who can’t read or have no data, with both seeker and poster sides.

Phase B · Later evolution on WhatsApp (not today’s POC call)

After the feature-phone path works, Meta can add smartphone WhatsApp numbers — still separate from the ordinary call path. Works on ordinary phone (no app required). WhatsApp option for those who have it, but not required.

  • Seeker WhatsApp number: Smartphone users message for nearby work; WhatsApp can capture location and details; AI searches FB Groups + IG Reels (+ later WhatsApp job posts) and replies in chat.
  • Employer / poster WhatsApp number (different number): Contractors post temp or permanent jobs by voice note or text. They do not see every seeker in an open chat.
  • Matching privacy: Seekers only see job matches from search; employers only see suitable candidates who posted looking for work on the seeker number. Each side gets the other’s contact only when matched — not a public dump of everyone.
  • Preferences: Ask both sides — e.g. employer: “Is out-of-state OK?” · seeker: “Open to relocate?”
  • Global scale: Same two-number pattern can separate posts and candidates by region, city, state, and country.
  • Three sources for seekers: FB Groups + IG Reels + WhatsApp gig posts — once the WhatsApp posting lane exists.

Scrimba walkthrough longevity + local assets
===========================================

Scrimba "Explain" links are interactive visual walkthroughs (not a single
downloadable MP4). yt-dlp cannot extract them. They remain playable via
embed as long as Scrimba hosts the guide and the claim token stays valid
(typically fine for a hackathon window; treat claim URLs as durable share
links, not forever archives).

If you later export or screen-capture real MP4s, place them here and the
site will prefer the local file automatically:

  assets/bada-how.mp4         -> landing + /how-it-works (Video 1)
  assets/bada-how-hi.mp4      -> optional Hindi capture for Video 1
  assets/bada-future.mp4      -> /future (Video 2)
  assets/bada-live-demo.mp4   -> /backup-demo English
  assets/bada-live-demo-hi.mp4-> /backup-demo Hindi

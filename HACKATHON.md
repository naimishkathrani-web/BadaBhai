# Hackathon positioning and demo checklist

## Project

BadaBhai brings practical voice AI to an ordinary phone call. It demonstrates a Hindi/English Exotel bridge to Meta Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Wonder, and local Meta MMS speech. The prototype answers everyday questions, explains topics simply, can repeat key points, expresses uncertainty, and directs callers to official sources for current public-service rules.

The demo is not a government service, eligibility decision tool, or personal-account assistant. The +91 79 4850 4917 line is a temporary Exotel judging number, not toll-free. Live Desk shows words only while a call is open. The app does not save the call.

## Demo priorities

1. Open the landing page and the 90-second walkthrough. If the temporary Exotel line is unavailable, use `/backup-demo`.
2. Open `/dashboard`, then call +91 79 4850 4917. Speak Hindi or English; the call auto-detects language and answers in kind. Talk over an answer to interrupt and continue.
3. Ask a simple everyday question such as “Photosynthesis kya hai, simple me samjhao?” Then ask for a public office address early in the call, before the free line’s short cutoff.
4. On Live Desk, show words, country code, language, timings, and interruptions only while the call is open.
5. Explain the limits honestly: one simultaneous call, Hindi/English only today, no saved calls, and future ideas are not live features.

## Impact framing

GSMA analysis estimates global annual feature-phone sales near 200 million in 2024, and a 2025 ETTelecom report attributes an approximately 54 million Indian shipment estimate to IDC. These are annual sales/shipment figures, not counts of active users. The landing page links the source material. Use exact claims with their source and year.

Potential directions include basic-phone learning, government program explanations from approved sources, natural-language enterprise IVR, voice ordering and logistics beyond cities, consent-led speech research for underrepresented languages, and live call translation. These are future opportunities, not working features or promised Meta capabilities. Do not claim that a named competitor lacks capabilities without evidence.

## Safety and data

The greeting does not mention recording or training. Live Desk is the only place words appear, and only during the open call. Do not ask for Aadhaar, OTP, bank PIN, or account credentials. Do not describe this call as a stored dataset.

Check the organizer's current rules, eligibility, schedule, license requirements, and submission fields directly before submitting. The judge call line and a completed video do not substitute for submission or acceptance. The participant remains responsible for accepting official terms and submitting the project.

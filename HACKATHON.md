# Participation and submission checklist

Source: participant guide supplied by the participant on 3 November 2026. This is a working checklist, not confirmation of acceptance or prize eligibility. Check official announcements before submitting.

## Model decision

Keep Meta Model API `muse-spark-1.3` for answers and `muse-voice-transcribe-1.0` for recognition. The guide permits the Muse Spark series through Meta Model API. **`meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026` is the Baseten-specific instruction**, not a universal required model ID. Do not send a Meta Model API credential to Baseten. Switching to Baseten would require its endpoint, a separate key and a fresh integration test.

Meta MMS generates speech locally. It is noncommercial CC-BY-NC-4.0; verify the intended submission, distribution and future business use against this license. MMS alone does not establish eligibility for the open-weight bonus: that award explicitly names Muse Glimmer or Muse Spark Open. Current configuration does not claim that bonus.

## Proposed positioning

- Theme: **Build for Your World**.
- Track: Indie Developer is appropriate if entering as an individual; participant must confirm.
- Project: voice access to practical information and digital services from basic phones, in Hindi and English.
- Show accessibility, keypad language selection, Meta speech recognition, spoken replies, multi-turn phone conversations and the Exotel bridge. The live judge dashboard surfaces transcript updates, selected language, source reference and first-response timings without displaying caller numbers.
- The focused PM-KISAN demonstration uses an official source and directs personal eligibility/status questions to the official channel. Pressing 3 after the answer requests an official-link SMS, but the feature only sends when the server has Exotel credentials and approved DLT configuration.
- Do not position the product as a medical/mental-health advisor, generic education chatbot, political/war assistant, screening system or another banned anti-project.
- Do not describe the current regular Exotel number as toll-free or claim global availability. Toll-free funding and expansion are future work.

## Demo priorities

Impact 30%, Meta tooling 25%, technical execution 20%, demo 25%. Start at the public judge landing page (`/`) and play the narrated walkthrough. For the live demo, have a basic-phone caller ask a concrete PM-KISAN question in natural Hindi or English and get concise, source-backed next steps. Keep `/operator` open to show the transcript, official source and time to first text/audio. Demonstrate keypad interruption and, only when DLT/SMS is configured and tested, press 3 for a consented text recap. Explain the real half-duplex limitation; don't present the browser text sandbox as the phone product or claim government approval. Advertise a call number only after Exotel confirms the public-facing route and international reachability.
